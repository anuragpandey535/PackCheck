1. 🏛️ Executive Summary & Legal Framework

PackCheck is an automated optical inspection and statutory auditing system designed for Legal Metrology Officers, Food & Civil Supplies Departments, and compliance auditors in India. It automatically verifies packaged goods against The Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (PCR 2011).

Statutory Rule Legal Requirement & Threshold PackCheck Automated Verification
Rule 6(1)(a) Name & Complete Postal Address of Manufacturer / Packer / Importer with valid PIN code. Extracts full address & checks PIN code presence.
Rule 6(1)(b) Generic or Common Commodity Name displayed prominently on Principal Display Panel (PDP). Validates generic commodity classification.
Rule 6(1)(c) & Rule 7 Net quantity in SI metric units (g, kg, ml, l, N) & minimum numeral font heights (Table I). Checks metric symbol syntax and font height vs PDP area.
Rule 6(1)(d) Month & Year of Manufacture / Pre-packing (e.g. MM/YYYY or PKD: MM/YY). Extracts manufacturing & packaging timelines.
Rule 6(1)(e) Maximum Retail Price (MRP) in Indian Rupees inclusive of all taxes. Verifies currency symbol and mandatory tax phrasing.
Rule 6(11) (2022 Mandate) Unit Sale Price (USP) declared in ₹ per gram/ml/piece for packages > standard threshold. Enforces USP presence (e.g., Rs. 0.25 / g).
Rule 6(10) Country of Origin clearly declared on imported packaged commodities. Flags missing origin on imported products.
Rule 6(1)(g) Consumer Grievance Redressal (Name, Postal Address, Phone Helpline, Email). Verifies multi-channel grievance contact information.
Fifth Schedule (PCR 2011) Mandatory statistical sample sizes based on total stock lot (20, 50, or 80 packets). Calculates statutory sample size before notice generation.

5. 🎯 Core Functional Modules

  • Batch Identification & Segregation: Scans thermal inkjet stamps on fin seals, crimps, and white date boxes to isolate B.No. / BN codes and organize stock into distinct batch cards with batch-level compliance scoring.
  • Fifth Schedule Statutory Sampling Calculator: Automatically enforces mandatory sample minimums (100–500 pkts → 20 units, 501–3200 pkts → 50 units, >3200 pkts → 80 units).
  • Official Legal Memorandum Generator: Produces formal print-ready inspection memorandums citing Section 36(1) and Rule 32 compounding options with isolated print stylesheet.
  • Dual-Layer Persistence Engine: Instant offline browser storage synchronized with backend disk storage (data/inspections.json).
